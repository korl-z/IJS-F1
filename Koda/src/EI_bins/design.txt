# EI_bins: steady-state solver on an energy shell grid

## Grid
- Cells are equally spaced in the shell variable s = cos kx + cos ky, i.e. in the bare energies. They never move with (d, m).
- The DOS weights come from the exact CDF of s, so there is no k-counting noise.
- Each cell has 4 sub-nodes for within-cell averages.
- ns is odd and chosen automatically: a cell spans at most min(d0, Tc, w0)/4 in quasiparticle energy.

## Pair kernel
- A_IJ = sum over k in I, p in J of w_k w_p phi(k - p), with phi(q) = q^2 / (q^2 + lam^2)^2 for |q| <= qd.
- It is computed by FFT on an L x L grid (L of about 250 to 1000) and cached.
- It is shared by all baths, temperatures, d and m.

## Rates
- Downhill: 2 pi |C|^2 (amp / w0) A [(1 + N) L(x - w0) + N L(x + w0)].
  - C is the coherence factor U_I^T c U_J, averaged over the cell pair.
  - L is the cell-pair overlap kernel: box * box * box, C1, with smoothing width at least the largest cell width.
- Uphill: downhill times exp(-x / T). Detailed balance is exact on the grid, so T1 = T2 reproduces the shell equilibrium exactly.
- Spectral-function extension: replace the bracket by the integral over w of L(x - w) B(w) (1 + N(w)).

## Unknowns and equations
- Unknowns: the GGE multipliers mu per (branch, cell), with n = 1 / (1 + e^mu), plus d, m and one slack kap per block.
- Balance: mu + log(in) - log(out) + kap = 0.
- Mean field: divided gap equation and the m equation, evaluated at sub-nodes. mu is interpolated linearly in E inside each cell, which is exact for thermal states and fixes the near-Tc error.
- Block populations: fixed in log form, as particles or holes, whichever is smaller.

## Solver
1. Inner Newton at fixed alpha/beta block populations.
   - Analytic mu Jacobian; central differences (h = 1e-7) for the d and m columns.
   - Merit weighted by sqrt(n (1 - n)).
   - Cells with |mu| > 50 are excluded from the convergence test.
2. Outer Newton on the block logits, driving 0.5 log(interband flux in / out) to zero.
   - Needed because interband rates can be 1e-8 of intraband ones.
3. Temperature homotopy from the exact T1 = T2 equilibrium, with a secant predictor.
   - If the ordered branch is lost, the normal branch is solved instead.
4. Stability from the Schur complement over (d, m) with instantaneous mean field. It also gives the slowest relaxation rate.

## Validation
- T1 = T2 steady state equals the equilibrium to 1e-11 to 1e-16.
- Particle number is conserved to 5e-17.
- The shell equilibrium converges to an independent k-grid equilibrium at O(h^2).
- Tc = 0.535236 (notebook: 0.53523542).

## Caveat
- For w0 < 2 d there is no interband channel. The alpha and beta populations are then separately conserved, and the steady state depends on the initial state. A finite phonon linewidth or another process is needed to fix them.
